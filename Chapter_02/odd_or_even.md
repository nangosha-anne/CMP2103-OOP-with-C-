# `odd_or_even.cpp`

## OOP Concepts Used

This program is procedural and does not use custom objects. It demonstrates a simple decision based on an arithmetic property.

## Algorithm

1. Read an integer.
2. Compute its remainder after division by two.
3. Report even when the remainder is zero; otherwise report odd.

## Possible Error Points

- The modulo test applies to integers, not fractional values.
- Negative integers still have a parity, but remainder comparisons should be chosen carefully.
- Reject or report invalid non-numeric input.
