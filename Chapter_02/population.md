# `population.cpp`

## OOP Concepts Used

This is a procedural calculation and does not define a population class. The input quantity and growth behavior could be encapsulated as state and methods in an OOP version.

## Algorithm

1. Read the initial population, growth rate, and time information.
2. Apply the given growth formula or repeated growth calculation.
3. Display the projected population.

## Possible Error Points

- Percentages must be converted to decimal rates before multiplication.
- Check whether the problem expects discrete yearly growth or a direct formula.
- Large projections can overflow an integer type.
